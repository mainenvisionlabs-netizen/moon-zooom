MOONZOOOM

vintage clothes, curiosities & a tiny museum of the strange

(yes, that’s three O’s)

Hi, we’re Moon Zooom.

We sell vintage clothing, and we keep it weird. Racks of 70s and 80s finds sit next to cases of oddities, old posters and things that belong in a museum.

The mood lands somewhere between a Cure record, a Smiths sleeve, a creepy late-night movie and a foggy afternoon at the beach. Hippie, alternative, vampire-ready and Santa Cruz to the bone.

Come in, dig through the racks, stay a while!
